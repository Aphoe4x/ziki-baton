#764 Mobile: unread notification badge on bottom navigation tab bar is misaligned for multi-digit counts

Description

Summary

On mobile, when an unread notification count exceeds a single digit (e.g. "12" or "99+"), the unread badge on the bottom navigation tab bar shifts off-center and clips outside the navigation icon container due to an improper negative right offset.

Steps to Reproduce

1. Log into a mobile workspace with more than 10 unread notifications or messages. 2. Observe the bottom navigation bar tab badges (e.g. DMs or Mentions tab).
